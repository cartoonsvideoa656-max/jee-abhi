JEE Main & Advanced

A complete guide to India's gateway for engineering: exam pattern, eligibility, syllabus, admission process and preparation strategy.

Overview

JEE Main

Conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It is the entrance for NITs, IIITs and other centrally funded technical institutes (CFTIs), and it is also the qualifying round for JEE Advanced.

JEE Advanced

Conducted by one of the IITs on a rotating basis. Only top JEE Main rankers qualify. It leads to admission into the IITs through JoSAA counselling.

Who takes it?

Lakhs of students appear every year, so a strong conceptual base in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics matters more than memorising.

JEE Main Paper Pattern

Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech)

  • 3 sections: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
  • 75 questions, 300 marks in total
  • Each subject: 20 MCQs + 5 numerical value questions
  • MCQ: +4 for correct, −1 for wrong
  • Numerical: +4 for correct, no negative marking

Paper 2A (B.Arch)

  • Mathematics, Aptitude Test and Drawing Test
  • Drawing is done on paper

Paper 2B (B.Planning)

  • Mathematics, Aptitude Test and Planning-based questions

The sessions are normally held in January and April. The result is given as NTA scores and percentiles, and the All India Rank is based on these.

JEE Advanced Paper Pattern

Structure

  • Two papers, each 3 hours, both compulsory
  • Each paper covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics

Question Types

  • Single-correct and multiple-correct MCQs
  • Numerical answer type
  • Paragraph / matching based questions

Marking

The pattern and marking scheme can change every year, and partial and negative marking often apply. Always read the current information brochure.

Qualifying for Advanced: roughly the top 2.5 lakh candidates of JEE Main Paper 1 are eligible, subject to the category-wise rules in the brochure.

Syllabus Highlights

Physics

  • Mechanics and Waves
  • Thermodynamics
  • Electrostatics and Current Electricity
  • Magnetism and EMI
  • Optics, Modern Physics

Chemistry

  • Physical: Mole concept, Equilibrium, Kinetics, Electrochemistry
  • Inorganic: Periodic table, Bonding, Coordination compounds
  • Organic: GOC, Reaction mechanisms, Biomolecules

Mathematics

  • Algebra: Quadratics, Sequences, Complex numbers
  • Calculus: Limits, Differentiation, Integration
  • Coordinate Geometry and Vectors
  • Probability and Statistics

Admission Process

1. Register

Apply for JEE Main on the NTA website with documents and fee.

2. Appear and Qualify

Take JEE Main. Top rankers register for JEE Advanced.

3. JoSAA Counselling

Fill choices of institutes and branches. Seats are allotted by rank, category and preference across rounds.

4. Report

Accept the seat, pay the fee and report with your documents.

Preparation Tips

Build Concepts

  • Finish NCERT first, especially Chemistry
  • Understand derivations rather than memorising

Practice

  • Solve previous year papers by topic
  • Take regular timed mock tests
  • Maintain an error notebook

Stay Consistent

  • Revise weekly and keep a fixed daily schedule
  • Sleep well and take short breaks

FAQ

Is JEE Main compulsory for IITs?

Yes. You must qualify in JEE Main Paper 1 to be eligible for JEE Advanced.

Is there an age limit?

JEE Main has no age limit. JEE Advanced has an age criterion that is stated in the yearly brochure.

Which subjects are required in Class 12?

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics are needed for B.Tech. Minimum marks criteria apply for admission to NITs/IITs, so check the brochure.

Can the pattern change?

Yes. Dates, marking and eligibility are updated every year, so confirm on the official sites before you apply.
